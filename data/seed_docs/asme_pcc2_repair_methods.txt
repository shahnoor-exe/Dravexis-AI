ASME PCC-2 Article 2.1 — Composite Reinforcement Repair Methods
Synthetic reference for MRPL equipment repair demonstration.

SECTION 1 — SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY

This article covers composite wrap repair systems for metallic pressure equipment with:
- Local internal/external corrosion defects
- Mechanical damage (dents, gouges) not exceeding 40% of wall thickness
- Operating temperature range: -29°C to +93°C (standard repair)
- Maximum operating pressure: per manufacturer qualification test

Inapplicable conditions (must use other methods):
- Defect exceeding 80% wall loss — requires metal insert or spool replacement
- Operating temperature >121°C — requires high-temperature composite qualification
- Leak-before-break defects — composite wraps are NOT leak seals; defect must be dry

SECTION 2 — DESIGN CALCULATION (ASME PCC-2 Eq. 2.1-3)

Minimum composite thickness t_c:
  t_c = (P × D) / (2 × S_c × E_c) - t_remaining

Where:
  P = operating gauge pressure (MPa)
  D = pipe outer diameter (mm)
  S_c = composite laminate allowable stress (MPa) from coupon testing
  E_c = composite repair efficiency factor (0.65 for manual wet layup; 0.80 for prepreg)
  t_remaining = remaining metal wall thickness (mm)

SYNTHETIC MRPL EXAMPLE — Line 4B-CS-150-N1A (CDU overhead):
  P = 0.95 MPa, D = 168.3 mm, t_nominal = 7.11 mm, t_remaining = 4.2 mm
  S_c = 48 MPa (glass/epoxy, wet layup), E_c = 0.65
  t_c = (0.95 × 168.3) / (2 × 48 × 0.65) - 4.2 = 2.55 mm (minimum composite layers = 4)

SECTION 3 — INSPECTION INTERVALS FOR REPAIRED PIPE

First inspection post-repair: 12 months (visual + acoustic emission)
Subsequent inspections: 3-year interval
Maximum qualified service life for composite repair: 20 years (per ISO 24817)
Condition for extension beyond 20 years: full recertification testing of witness coupons
