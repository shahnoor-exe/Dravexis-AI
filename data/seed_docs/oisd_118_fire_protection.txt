OISD Standard 118 — Layouts for Oil and Gas Installations
Third Revision — Section 8: Fire Protection Systems (Illustrative Excerpt for Demo Purposes)

8. FIRE PROTECTION IN PETROLEUM REFINERIES

8.1 SCOPE

This section covers the minimum requirements for fire detection, alarm, and suppression systems in petroleum refineries, including fixed water spray systems, foam systems, and gas suppression systems for enclosed spaces.

8.2 WATER SPRAY SYSTEMS FOR PRESSURE VESSELS AND STORAGE TANKS

8.2.1 Fixed water spray systems shall be provided for all pressure vessels containing flammable hydrocarbons above their auto-ignition temperature or operating above 60°C with a design pressure exceeding 10 kg/cm².

8.2.2 The minimum water application rate for cooling of pressure vessels exposed to fire shall be:
    - Wetted surface: 10.2 L/min/m² (0.25 gpm/ft²)
    - Unwetted surface (drainage plane): 4.1 L/min/m² (0.10 gpm/ft²)

8.2.3 The water spray system for a vessel shall be capable of operating for a minimum duration of 60 MINUTES without replenishment from external sources.

8.3 FOAM SYSTEMS FOR ATMOSPHERIC STORAGE TANKS

8.3.1 Fixed foam systems (top-pouring or sub-surface injection) shall be provided for all atmospheric storage tanks with a capacity exceeding 1,000 KL containing Class A or Class B petroleum products.

8.3.2 The foam solution application rate shall be:
    - Class A products (flash point < 23°C): Minimum 6.5 L/min/m² of liquid surface area
    - Class B products (23°C ≤ flash point < 65°C): Minimum 4.1 L/min/m² of liquid surface area

8.3.3 Foam concentrate quantity shall be sufficient for 30 MINUTES of continuous operation at the design application rate, with a 100% backup supply maintained on site.

8.4 FIRE DETECTION SYSTEMS

8.4.1 A combination of point-type heat detectors, UV/IR flame detectors, and combustible gas detectors shall be installed in all process areas.

8.4.2 Combustible gas detectors shall be set to alarm at 10% of the Lower Explosive Limit (LEL) and trip safety systems at 20% LEL.

8.4.3 The fire and gas detection system shall be integrated with the Safety Instrumented System (SIS) and shall initiate automatic shutdown of relevant process units upon confirmed detection.

8.5 SPACING REQUIREMENTS — SAFETY DISTANCES

Minimum safety distances between process equipment in petroleum refineries:
    - Fired heater to flammable liquid storage: 30 m
    - Compressor to fired heater: 15 m
    - Flare stack to process equipment: 60 m (based on radiation calculation, whichever is greater)
    - Control room to process unit boundary: 30 m minimum; blast-resistant design if < 60 m

8.6 PASSIVE FIRE PROTECTION

All structural steel within 15 m of fired equipment or storage tanks containing Class A/B products shall be provided with fireproofing rated for a minimum of TWO (2) HOURS at 1093°C (UL 1709 or equivalent standard).
