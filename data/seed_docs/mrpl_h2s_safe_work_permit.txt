MRPL H2S Safe Work Permit Procedure — SWP-H2S-002 Rev 5
Synthetic internal permit for demonstration purposes only.

1. SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY
This procedure governs all activities in areas classified as H2S-risk zones within MRPL.
Applies to: maintenance, inspection, sampling, and construction activities in CDU, HCU, and FCC.

2. H2S RISK CLASSIFICATION

Zone Class | H2S Concentration | PPE Minimum  | Entry Requirement
Class I    | 0–1 ppm           | H2S detector | Normal access with detector
Class II   | 1–10 ppm          | SCBA standby | Permit required, 2-person rule
Class III  | >10 ppm           | SCBA mandatory | Hot work restricted; emergency only

3. PERMIT REQUIREMENTS

Standard H2S Work Permit (SWP-H2S-002):
- Issuer: Area Operations Technician + Safety Officer countersign
- Validity: 12 hours per shift; must be renewed for each shift
- Wind sock check: mandatory before entry — work facing upwind
- Rescue plan: identified standby with SCBA before permit issue

Continuous gas monitoring requirements:
- Portable H2S detector: calibrated within 30 days
- Fixed detectors must be operational and alarming at 1 ppm (first alarm)
- At 10 ppm alarm: work stops, non-essential personnel evacuate, supervisor notified
