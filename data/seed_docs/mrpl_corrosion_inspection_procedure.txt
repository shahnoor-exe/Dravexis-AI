MRPL Corrosion Inspection Procedure — COR-INSP-001 Rev 3
Synthetic internal procedure document for demonstration purposes only.

1. SCOPE
This procedure covers corrosion inspection methodology for static equipment in MRPL refinery process units:
CDU (Crude Distillation Unit), Hydrocracker (HCU), and FCC (Fluid Catalytic Cracker).

2. INSPECTION TECHNIQUES (prioritised by defect type)

2.1 Ultrasonic Thickness Measurement (UTM)
Equipment: Krautkramer DM5E with V103 transducer (5 MHz, straight beam)
Grid spacing: 50 mm × 50 mm in general areas; 25 mm × 25 mm at weld toes
Minimum scan area: all grid points within 300 mm of any CML (Corrosion Monitoring Location)
Acceptance: measured thickness ≥ t_min (per ASME B31.3 design calculation)

2.2 Phased Array Ultrasonic Testing (PAUT)
Use when: pitting index > 0.3 (scattered pitting detected by UTM)
Equipment: Olympus OmniScan MX2, 5L16-A10 probe
Resolution: ≤ 1 mm lateral; ≤ 0.5 mm depth

2.3 Automated Ultrasonic (AUT) Scanning for CUI (Corrosion Under Insulation)
Trigger: pipe operating temperature 60–120°C in wet climate zone
Method: LRUT (Long Range Ultrasonic Testing) for screening, UTM for confirmation

Inspection intervals apply per OISD 116 risk classification:
- Risk Class A (H2S wet service): 5-year inspection interval, 2-year UT spot check
- Risk Class B (Non-lethal service): 10-year inspection interval, 5-year UT spot check
- Risk Class C (Low energy, ambient): 15-year inspection interval

4. SAFE WORK REQUIREMENTS
- Gas test mandatory before entry: O2 ≥ 19.5%, LEL ≤ 10%, H2S ≤ 1 ppm TWA
- Scaffold inspection certificate required if > 3 m elevation
- Radiation permit required for gamma radiography; 15 m exclusion zone mandatory
