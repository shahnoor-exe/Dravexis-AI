ASME B31.3 — Process Piping Code
2022 Edition — Chapter II: Design (Illustrative Excerpt for Demo Purposes)

302. DESIGN CONDITIONS

302.1 Design Pressure

302.1.1 The design pressure of each component in a piping system shall be not less than the maximum sustained operating pressure (MSOP) including the effect of static head of fluid where significant.

302.1.2 For systems subject to pressure surges (water hammer, pump shutdown), the design pressure shall account for the maximum surge pressure.

302.2 Design Temperature

302.2.1 The design temperature for each component shall be the temperature at which the component is designed to operate, based on the maximum expected fluid or ambient temperature, whichever is greater.

302.2.2 For uninsulated components, the metal temperature may be taken as 85% of the maximum process fluid temperature unless calculated otherwise by heat transfer analysis.

302.2.3 The minimum design temperature shall be the lowest temperature at which the piping is required to be capable of carrying its design pressure.

303. PRESSURE DESIGN OF COMPONENTS

303.1 Straight Pipe Under Internal Pressure

The minimum required wall thickness for straight pipe under internal pressure is given by:

    t_m = (P × D) / (2 × (SE + PY))   [Equation 3a]

    Where:
        t_m = Minimum required wall thickness (mm or in)
        P   = Internal design pressure (MPa or psi)
        D   = Outside diameter of pipe (mm or in)
        S   = Allowable stress value from Table A-1 (MPa or psi)
        E   = Longitudinal joint quality factor (dimensionless, from Table A-1B)
        Y   = Coefficient from Table 304.1.1 (temperature-dependent)

    For temperatures below 480°C (900°F), Y = 0.4 for ferritic steels.
    For temperatures 480°C to 565°C, Y = 0.5.
    For temperatures above 565°C, Y = 0.7.

303.2 Wall Thickness Tolerance

The specified wall thickness of pipe shall be the minimum required thickness t_m plus a mill tolerance of 12.5% (for seamless pipe) or as stated in the applicable pipe specification.

304. ALLOWABLE STRESS AND TEMPERATURE LIMITS

304.1 Pressure-Temperature Ratings

Components conforming to listed standards (e.g., ASME B16.5 flanges) may be used at pressures and temperatures not exceeding those tabulated in the applicable standard for the material and rating class.

304.1.1 For ASME B16.5 Class 300 flanges in carbon steel (Group 1.1):
    - Maximum allowable pressure at 38°C (100°F): 51.1 bar (740 psi)
    - Maximum allowable pressure at 260°C (500°F): 41.4 bar (600 psi)
    - Maximum allowable pressure at 454°C (850°F): 26.2 bar (380 psi)

304.2 High-Temperature Limitations

Process piping operating above 427°C (800°F) shall be subjected to mandatory creep analysis per Appendix V of ASME B31.3. The material shall demonstrate adequate rupture ductility as per ASME Section II, Part D.

305. FLEXIBILITY AND SUPPORT

305.1 A piping system shall have sufficient flexibility to prevent thermal expansion or contraction from causing pipe failure by overstress, damage to connected equipment, or leakage at joints.

305.2 Expansion loops, offsets, or flexible connections shall be provided where the calculated sustained stress (SL) combined with thermal expansion stress (SE) exceeds the allowable stress range SA:

    SA = f × (1.25 × Sc + 0.25 × Sh)

    Where:
        Sc = Basic allowable stress at cold (installation) temperature
        Sh = Basic allowable stress at hot (operating) temperature
        f  = Stress range reduction factor (1.0 for ≤7,000 cycles)
