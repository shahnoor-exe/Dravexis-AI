SECTION A — FIXED GAS DETECTOR PLACEMENT (OISD-118 Annex B)

B.1 General Requirements
Fixed combustible gas detectors shall be installed in enclosed or semi-enclosed areas where flammable gas or vapour can accumulate.
Detector spacing: Maximum 5 metres apart in high-risk zones; 10 metres in low-risk zones.
Coverage per detector head: 50 m² (H2S service); 80 m² (HC vapour service).

B.2 Alarm Setpoints (OISD-118 Table B-2)
First alarm (alert): 10% LEL (Lower Explosive Limit)
Second alarm (action): 20% LEL
Emergency shutdown trigger: 40% LEL
Toxic gas (H2S) first alarm: 1 ppm (TLV-TWA)
H2S second alarm: 10 ppm
H2S IDLH threshold: 50 ppm

B.3 Detector Technology Selection
Areas with H2S service: Electrochemical cells mandatory; catalytic bead NOT acceptable due to H2S poisoning.
Areas with methane/propane service: Catalytic bead acceptable; IR open-path preferred in large open areas.
Voting logic: 1-of-2 for alert; 2-of-3 for emergency shutdown in safety-critical areas.

SECTION B — HEAT DETECTOR PLACEMENT (OISD-118 Annex C)

C.1 Rate-of-rise detectors: Sensitive to 8.5°C/min rise rate; trigger at absolute 57°C.
C.2 Fixed temperature detectors: 68°C in process buildings; 93°C in pump houses.
C.3 Hydrocarbon pool fire: Response time requirement ≤ 30 seconds to first alarm.
